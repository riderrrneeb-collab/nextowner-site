Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

NextOwner ("NextOwner," "we," "us") runs mynextowner.com, a marketplace where people list small businesses for sale and buyers browse them. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have. If you have questions, email mynextownerofficial@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account or log in

  • Email login: your email address, which we use to send you a sign-in code or link.
  • Sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.

When you list a business

  • The details you enter: business name and type, city, state and ZIP code, asking price, revenue, cash flow, description, video tour link, and the contact name, email and phone number buyers should use.
  • Photos you upload from your device or choose from Google Drive. We resize photos and save them as JPEG files. Resizing may remove embedded photo data such as camera details and location.
  • An approximate map location for your listing, worked out from the ZIP code you enter.

When you buy a plan

Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card or other payment details; NextOwner never sees or stores your full card number. We receive your email address, which plan you bought, and whether your subscription is active.

When you verify your identity

ID checks are run by Stripe Identity. Stripe collects images of your government-issued ID and a selfie and checks that they match. NextOwner does not receive or store your ID images or selfie. We receive the result of the check (for example, "verified") and use it to show the ✓ Verified badge on your listings. Stripe's handling of this data is described in the Stripe Privacy Policy.

When you use Best Picks

The budget, location, distance and preferences you enter are used in your browser to rank listings for you.

Automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use advertising trackers.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To publish, display and promote your listings, including on our social media channels if your plan includes social promotion.
  • To let logged-in buyers contact sellers about a listing.
  • To manage subscriptions, apply plan features and show verification badges.
  • To prevent fraud, scams and misuse, and to enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To answer your questions and send messages about your account, such as sign-in codes and billing notices.
  • To improve the site, including from feedback you email us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

3. Google user data

NextOwner uses Google services in two ways:

  • Sign in with Google requests your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) so we can create and log in to your NextOwner account.
  • Add from Google Drive lets you choose photos from your Drive for a listing. It uses Google's drive.file permission, which only gives NextOwner access to the specific files you pick in the Google Picker. We cannot see, list or open any other files in your Drive. We download the photos you pick, resize them and save them as photos on your listing. The temporary access token Google provides stays in your browser and is not stored on our servers.

We use Google user data only to provide these features. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide the service (such as storing your listing photos with our hosting provider) or as required by law, and do not allow humans to read it except with your consent, for security purposes or as required by law. NextOwner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove NextOwner's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Photos you already added to a listing stay on that listing until you remove them or delete the listing.

4. Who we share information with

We use trusted service providers to run NextOwner. They process information on our behalf:

  • Netlify: website hosting, server functions and storage of listings and photos.
  • Supabase: account login (email codes, links and Google sign-in).
  • Stripe: payments, subscriptions, the billing portal and identity verification.
  • Google: Sign in with Google and the Google Drive photo picker.
  • Zippopotam.us: turns a listing's ZIP code into an approximate map location. Only the ZIP code is sent.
  • Content networks (jsDelivr, Unsplash): deliver code libraries and the photos on example listings. Like any website, they receive your IP address when your browser loads these files.

We may also share information if required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of NextOwner, our users or others, and as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

5. What other people can see

  • Anyone visiting the site can see a live listing's business details, location (city, state and map area), financials, description, photos and video, and whether it has a ✓ Verified badge.
  • Logged-in users can see the contact name, email and phone number you put on a listing when they choose to contact you.

Only put information on a listing that you are comfortable sharing publicly. Drafts are not public.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser's local storage, not advertising cookies, to keep you logged in, remember that you closed the beta notice, and remember what you were doing when you leave for checkout. Stripe and Google may set their own cookies on their pages to run checkout, sign-in and fraud prevention. You can clear this storage in your browser settings; you will be logged out.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your account and listing information while your account is active. You can edit or delete your listings at any time from your account page; deleting a listing deletes its photos. To delete your account, email us from the address on your account and we will delete your account and listings within 30 days, except for records we must keep for legal, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes (for example, payment records held by Stripe).

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), keep secret keys on our servers rather than in the website code, and limit who can change listings. No online service can be completely secure, so please use a secure email account and let us know right away if you think your account has been misused.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to get a copy of it, by emailing mynextownerofficial@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example, California or Colorado), you may have additional rights under local law. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

10. Children

NextOwner is for adults buying and selling businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as NextOwner changes. We will change the date at the top, and if a change is significant we will let account holders know by email or on the site before it takes effect.

12. Contact us

Email mynextownerofficial@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.
